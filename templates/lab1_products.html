📖 Vulnerability Explanation

Vulnerable Code:

query = f"SELECT * FROM products WHERE category='{category}' AND released=1"
cursor.execute(query)

Safe Code:

query = "SELECT * FROM products WHERE category=? AND released=1"
cursor.execute(query, (category,))

Attack: Input ' OR 1=1-- changes the query to WHERE category='' OR 1=1--' AND released=1. The -- comments out the rest, and OR 1=1 is always true, returning ALL products including hidden ones.
